# Demo Script: SPARK Platform

**Total Time:** ~3:00  
**Narrator:** Platform Champion  
**Target Audience:** Freshers, Mentors, and Stakeholders

---

## Scene 1: Home page tour
**Timestamp:** 0:00 - 0:20  
**Screen Action:** Slowly scroll down the Home page. Cursor highlights the Hero banner, stops at Quick Stats, then pans across the Featured Opportunities gallery.  
**Narration (Voice-over):** "Welcome to SPARK, Visteon's new hub for accelerating careers and finding hands-on technical opportunities. On the homepage, you immediately see the latest highlighted projects and quick stats on platform engagement."  
**Transition Note:** Crossfade to the Explore page.

## Scene 2: Explore
**Timestamp:** 0:20 - 0:40  
**Screen Action:** Click on 'Explore' in the top nav. On the page, click the filter dropdown for 'Tech Stack' and select 'AI/ML'. The gallery filters. Hover over a specific card.  
**Narration:** "Looking to build specific skills? Head to the Explore page. Here, Freshers can filter by tech stack—like AI/ML—and browse beautifully formatted cards to find the perfect match."  
**Transition Note:** Click on a project card to open Details.

## Scene 3: Opportunity details
**Timestamp:** 0:40 - 0:55  
**Screen Action:** Scroll through the details page showing full project description, required skills, and the mentor's profile card.  
**Narration:** "Clicking a card takes you to the Opportunity Details. You'll see exactly what the project entails and learn more about the Mentor you'll be working with."  
**Transition Note:** Scroll down to the Apply section.

## Scene 4: Apply Now
**Timestamp:** 0:55 - 1:15  
**Screen Action:** Click the 'Apply Now' button (opens a customized SharePoint list form or Microsoft Form). Fill in dummy text for 'Why I'm a fit' and hit Submit. A success toast appears.  
**Narration:** "Ready to jump in? Submitting an application is seamless. Just fill out a brief statement of interest and hit apply. Your profile data is captured automatically."  
**Transition Note:** Cut to an Outlook Inbox view.

## Scene 5: Manager gets email -> opens Manager Hub
**Timestamp:** 1:15 - 1:35  
**Screen Action:** Show an Outlook email arriving with the subject "New SPARK Application". Click the deep link in the email which opens the 'Manager Hub' SharePoint page.  
**Narration:** "Behind the scenes, Power Automate immediately notifies the Mentor. By clicking the link in their email, they're taken directly to their customized Manager Hub to review applicants."  
**Transition Note:** Focus on the Applications list view in Manager Hub.

## Scene 6: Manager shortlists applicant
**Timestamp:** 1:35 - 1:55  
**Screen Action:** In the list view, the Manager clicks the 'Status' dropdown for the new applicant and changes it from 'Pending' to 'Shortlisted'.  
**Narration:** "The Mentor reviews the application and updates the status to 'Shortlisted' with a single click, triggering the next workflow."  
**Transition Note:** Cut to Fresher's Outlook/Teams.

## Scene 7: Applicant gets notification email
**Timestamp:** 1:55 - 2:10  
**Screen Action:** Show the Fresher receiving an adaptive card in Teams or an email: "Congratulations! You've been shortlisted for..."  
**Narration:** "The Fresher instantly receives a notification celebrating their shortlisted status, keeping communication transparent and fast."  
**Transition Note:** Fast forward visual effect. Back to Manager Hub.

## Scene 8: Mark opportunity Completed -> Rewards draft created
**Timestamp:** 2:10 - 2:35  
**Screen Action:** The Manager edits the main Opportunity item, changing Status to 'Completed'. Briefly flash the Rewards list showing a new draft entry auto-created.  
**Narration:** "Fast forward—the project is a success! When the Mentor marks the opportunity as Completed, the system automatically drafts a Reward entry to grant internal badges to the successful Fresher."  
**Transition Note:** Cut to Hall of Fame page.

## Scene 9: Winner appears in Hall of Fame
**Timestamp:** 2:35 - 2:50  
**Screen Action:** Scroll down the Hall of Fame page, revealing the Fresher's photo and the project they just completed in a spotlight web part.  
**Narration:** "And to celebrate our talent, the completed project and the Fresher's achievements are automatically showcased on our Hall of Fame for the whole company to see."  
**Transition Note:** Fade to SPARK logo.

## Scene 10: Outro
**Timestamp:** 2:50 - 3:00  
**Screen Action:** Static or gently pulsing SPARK logo with the text: "SPARK: Ignite Your Potential."  
**Narration:** "SPARK. Ignite your potential, build your network, and shape the future at Visteon."  
**Transition Note:** Fade to black.
