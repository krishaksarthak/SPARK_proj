Frequently Asked Questions

1. Finding Opportunities

Use the filters on the Explore page. You can filter by 'Technology' tags such as React, Python, Power BI, etc., or use the keyword search bar.

Yes, you can have up to 3 active applications at any given time. Once an application is closed (accepted/rejected/completed), you can apply for more.

2. Application Process

The mentor who posted the opportunity gets notified immediately via email and Teams. Your current line manager will also receive a courtesy notification.

Mentors typically review applications within 5 business days. You can track your application status in the 'My Applications' tab.

3. For Managers & Mentors

Navigate to the 'Manager Hub' and click 'New Opportunity'. Fill in the project details, required skills, and effort needed, then submit for approval.

In the Manager Hub, click on your active opportunity to see the applicant list. You can review their profiles and change their status to 'Interviewing', 'Accepted', or 'Rejected'.

4. Recognition & Rewards

Upon successful completion of a project, the mentor assesses the contribution. Exceptional contributions are nominated for the monthly SPARK Innovation Awards.

Yes, peer nominations are accepted through the 'Rewards' page, subject to validation by the project mentor.

5. Technical Help

Access to the Manager Hub is restricted to users with Manager or Mentor roles. If you need access, please request it via the IT Service Portal.

If an application is stuck, please reach out to the opportunity mentor directly via Teams, or contact the SPARK Admin team.
