EV Dashboard POC with Power BI

Open Innovation ⭐ Featured

We are initiating a Proof of Concept (POC) to build a real-time Electrical Vehicle (EV) Dashboard using Power BI. This project aims to visualize telemetry data extracted from the CAN bus and OBD-II interfaces of our test vehicles. The dashboard will help engineering teams monitor battery health, motor efficiency, and thermal management metrics in real-time.

As a contributor to this project, you will be responsible for setting up the data pipelines from Azure IoT Hub to Power BI, and designing interactive dashboards that highlight critical KPIs. You will work closely with the Connected Services team to understand the nuances of CAN protocol data and how it translates to actionable insights.

This is a fantastic opportunity for someone looking to deepen their expertise in data visualization within the automotive domain, working on cutting-edge EV technology.
